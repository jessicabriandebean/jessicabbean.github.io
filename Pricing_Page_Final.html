Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What files do you convert?

We convert Microsoft Publisher (.pub) files to editable Word documents, PDFs, and other formats. We preserve all formatting, images, and layouts.

❓ How long does conversion take?

Standard timeline is 5-7 business days after we receive your files. Rush options available for urgent projects.

❓ Should I schedule a consultation?

Yes! Consultations are free and help us understand your unique needs. Highly recommended for complex projects or if you're unsure which package fits.

❓ What's the difference between packages?

Basic: Just the conversion. Standard: Includes mail merge setup. Premium: Full service with training and support.

❓ How do I pay?

50% deposit to start, 50% when we deliver. We accept credit cards, bank transfers, and checks.

❓ What if I need revisions?

Unlimited revisions are included! If you want changes, just let us know and we'll update your files at no extra cost.

❓ Do you offer training?

Yes! Premium package includes 30 minutes of team training. Basic and Standard packages can add training for an additional fee.
